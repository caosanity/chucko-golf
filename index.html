Why not leather

What makes this glove different?

Leather absorbs moisture, stiffens in the cold, and breaks down unevenly as it dries. Microfiber doesn't. It's the material Japanese golf brands have engineered - and tour pros there have trusted - for years. We're just the first to bring it to Canada.
