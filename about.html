About us

Meet Charles - our resident cockapoo, and the reason this brand exists.

Jeff - Founder & Owner
Charles - Full-time mascot, Part-time quality control

Rain or shine, snow or scorching heat, Charles is out there in it, tail up, completely unbothered by whatever Canadian weather throws at him. That same all-weather resilience is exactly what we wanted in a golf glove - because if you golf in Canada, you already know the forecast can change four times before you finish the front nine.

Here's the thing about most golf gloves on the market: brands find a manufacturer overseas, put a leather glove in a bag, slap their logo on it, and call it done. Leather looks and feels great - until conditions change. It gets slick and loses grip in the humidity and rain, stiffens up and loses feel in the cold, and turns sweaty and warm the second temperatures climb. It's a material built for ideal conditions, not the ones we actually play in.

Microfiber solves this - and it's not some unproven idea. It's the material tour pros in Japan already reach for, engineered and trusted in one of the most demanding golf markets in the world. It's just never been brought to Canada as anything more than a budget alternative. We think that's backwards. So we went a different direction: built our glove around premium microfiber from day one, treating it as the upgrade it actually is - grippy in the wet, breathable in the heat, and flexible instead of stiff when temperatures drop.

Just like Charles - dependable, playful, and ready for whatever the day brings - we wanted a glove that performs no matter the conditions. That's the whole idea behind this brand: bring a proven, premium material to Canadian golf, instead of settling for the sunny-day version of it.

- Jeff, Founder & Owner
