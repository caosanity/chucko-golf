Returns & exchanges

Get the fit right the first time.

We're a small, new brand, and right now we're not able to offer returns or exchanges for sizing or fit. Because of this, we'd rather you take the extra minute upfront to get your size right than deal with a glove that doesn't fit. Check our sizing guide before you order - if you're between sizes or unsure, message us and we'll help you figure out the right fit before you buy.

01

Check sizing first

Golf glove sizing runs differently than you'd expect. Use our size guide (hand circumference, not glove brand history) before ordering.

02

Ask before you order

Not sure which size fits? Send us a message on [Instagram/TikTok/email] before you order - we're happy to help you figure it out.

03

All sales final

Because we're a new, small operation, all sales are currently final for sizing or change of mind. We're working toward offering exchanges as we grow - thanks for understanding and supporting an early-stage brand.

Defective item?

If your glove arrives damaged or defective, contact us with photos within 3 days of receiving your order and we'll make it right.
